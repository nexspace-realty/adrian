The gap

Sindhorn Tonson gives you 25 sqm more and a balcony for 45,000 THB more each month. Romm Convent is newer, higher up and a shorter walk to the BTS.

Both units: bathtub, water heater, air-conditioning, TV and full kitchen. Utilities are paid separately in both buildings.
